# Wireframes prompt

Paste in a fresh design session with the brief attached. Replace the
angle-bracket parts.

---

Produce low-fidelity wireframes for <Product>, a <platform> app that
<one-sentence purpose>. Audience: <who>. Attached: the brief.

Scope: these screens, in flow order: <list, numbered>. Nothing else.

Fidelity: structure only. Gray boxes, real labels, real content lengths,
no color, no icons beyond a placeholder square, system font. The point is
hierarchy, grouping and navigation, not look.

Deliver a single `Wireframes.html` that opens by double-click: one
column per screen, device-sized frames, arrows in text between screens
describing the navigation. Add a short annotation under each screen:
purpose, primary action, what is deliberately absent.

Rules I hold to:

- One primary action per screen.
- Every piece of personal data shown has a reason on that screen.
- Empty, loading and error states drawn for the screens that have them.
- Platform chrome where the platform provides it: tab bar, navigation
  bar, sheets. Do not invent navigation.
- No lorem ipsum. Realistic names, dates, amounts that add up.

Before drawing, ask me one or two questions about what is already decided
(flows, constraints, business rules). Then propose the screen list with a
one-line purpose each and wait for my go.

After validation I will ask for two or three visual directions, then a
hi-fi prototype. Keep the wireframe markup simple enough to evolve.
